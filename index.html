// Built with safety in mind

Trust isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

A health platform earns trust by design, not by claim.

🩺
AI doesn't replace doctors. Every AI response is guidance, not diagnosis — and serious cases are routed to real, verified doctors.
🚨
Emergency situations are escalated. Panic Mode and Quick Triage surface real emergency numbers immediately when symptoms suggest urgency.
✅
Doctor credentials are verified. Every doctor's license is manually reviewed before they can see a single patient.
🔒
Your health information is protected. Profile data, vitals, and conversations are only ever visible to you and doctors you're actively speaking with.

AI responses are clearly identified. You always know when you're talking to an AI versus a human doctor — never ambiguous.

MedAI is not a replacement for professional medical care

MedAI provides AI-generated health information and guidance for educational purposes only. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace the advice of a licensed healthcare professional. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency services immediately. Always consult a qualified doctor for diagnosis and treatment decisions.
