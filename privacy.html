Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED — AUGUST 2026 (DRAFT)

This Privacy Policy explains what information MedAI collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. MedAI provides AI-powered health guidance and connects patients with verified doctors.

1. Information we collect

Account information: name, email, and password (or Google account, if you sign in with Google), and whether you're registering as a patient or a doctor.

Health profile: date of birth, gender, height, weight, blood group, conditions, allergies, medications, lifestyle details (smoking, alcohol, exercise), and emergency contact — all optional, all provided by you.

Vitals: heart rate, SpO2, temperature, blood pressure, glucose, and weight readings you log manually or via the in-app camera scanner.

Symptom history: Quick Triage results, including what you typed and the AI's assessment.

Conversations: messages and images exchanged with AI assistants and with doctors you're actively speaking with.

Doctor-specific information (for doctor accounts): license number, issuing authority, specialty, years of experience, hospital/clinic affiliation, and workplace location.

Payment information: if you upgrade to Premium, payment is processed by our payment provider, Bachs — MedAI never sees or stores your card details.

2. How we use this information

  • To provide AI health guidance across our Medical, Safe Space, Mental, and Physical Health assistants
  • To match you with an appropriate, available doctor when your symptoms suggest one is needed
  • To let doctors you're actively speaking with see relevant health context — but only doctors you've started a conversation with, never the full doctor network
  • To show doctors their patients' vitals, symptom history, and profile so they can give informed guidance
  • To send you notifications about messages, appointments, and account activity
  • To process Premium subscription payments

3. Third parties we work with

MedAI uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Google — for account sign-in (Google OAuth)
  • Google Gemini and OpenRouter — to power our AI health assistants. Your messages to these assistants are processed by these providers to generate responses.
  • Bachs — to process Premium subscription payments
  • OpenStreetMap — to show doctor locations and search addresses on maps

We do not sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Who can see your information

Your health profile, vitals, and symptom history are visible to you and to doctors you have an active conversation with — never to the wider doctor network, and never to other patients. Doctor reviews and ratings you leave are visible to other patients considering that doctor.

5. Your rights

You can update or delete your profile information at any time from your account settings. You may request full account deletion by contacting us — see below.

6. Security

Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text. Access to your account requires authentication, and doctor-patient conversations are only visible to the two parties involved.

7. Children's privacy

MedAI is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MedAI evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
