1. Agreement to Terms

2. Description of Service

MedAI provides AI-powered health assistants (Medical, Safe Space, Mental, and Physical Health), AI-assisted symptom triage, a personal vitals and symptom history, a Panic Mode with guided breathing exercises, a Medical Locator for nearby healthcare facilities, and tools to connect and communicate with independent doctors, including appointments and a Premium tier with expanded AI usage. A detailed account of what information each feature collects is in our Privacy Policy, which this document should be read alongside.

MedAI is not a substitute for professional medical care. MedAI provides AI-generated health information for informational and supportive purposes only. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a licensed healthcare professional. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately — do not rely on MedAI in an emergency.

3. Medical Disclaimer

Nothing generated by MedAI's AI assistants, or produced by Quick Triage, constitutes a medical diagnosis, treatment plan, or professional medical advice. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Always use your own judgment, and consult a qualified healthcare professional for any health concern — especially before starting, stopping, or changing any medication or treatment. Triage results indicating urgency are a prompt to seek appropriate care, not a substitute for it.

7. Doctor-Patient Relationship and Data Access

MedAI is a platform that helps patients and doctors connect and communicate — MedAI is not itself a party to the doctor-patient relationship, does not practice medicine, and does not guarantee the outcome of any consultation. Our Privacy Policy describes the technical access model in detail, including that a patient controls whether a doctor can see their fuller profile, vitals, and triage history via a "Share Profile" toggle they can turn off at any time.

Current limitation: as described in our Privacy Policy, there is currently no way to fully end a conversation or remove a doctor's access to chat messages already exchanged — turning off "Share Profile" only affects profile, vitals, and triage-history visibility going forward.

8. AI-Generated Content

Responses from MedAI's AI assistants are generated by third-party AI providers — currently Google Gemini as the primary provider, with OpenRouter (auto-routed to a variable underlying model) as a fallback if Gemini is unavailable. AI responses are not reviewed by a human before being shown to you, are provided for informational purposes, and are clearly not a medical diagnosis or treatment plan. AI output is provided "as is," without any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

9. Automated Triage and Doctor Matching

Quick Triage uses AI to classify the symptoms you describe and may suggest connecting you with an available doctor of a matching specialty, or with the Mental Health assistant. This classification is automated and may be inaccurate or incomplete. It does not replace your own judgment about whether and when to seek in-person or emergency care.

12. Premium Subscriptions and Payments

Premium unlocks expanded AI usage and is paid for through our payment provider, Bachs. As currently implemented, purchasing Premium is a one-time payment that grants a fixed access period — 30 days for the monthly plan, 365 days for the yearly plan — starting from the date of successful payment.

Correcting a claim in the previous version of these terms: Premium does not currently auto-renew. There is no stored payment method and no recurring charge — MedAI does not automatically bill you again when your access period ends. Premium access simply lapses at the end of the paid period unless you choose to purchase another one. Because there is no auto-renewal, there is correspondingly no "cancel subscription" action needed or available.

Pricing varies by the country you select at checkout and is shown to you before payment. Payment itself is processed entirely on Bachs' hosted checkout page — MedAI's servers never receive or store your card details (see our Privacy Policy, Section 10).
